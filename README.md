# ChemCorp Inc. – Executive Customer Segmentation & Divestment Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview
ChemCorp Inc., a Fortune 500 chemical producer, has experienced an impressive **7% Year-on-Year market growth**, leading to expansions across 48 US states. However, lowered government regulations have introduced aggressive new startups leveraging machine learning and AI to lower production costs. 

Recently, **five long-term customers defected to competitors, resulting in an immediate 10% loss in total sales revenue**. To prevent further market erosion and safeguard shareholder dividend payments, this business analytics project answers a critical directive from Chief Sales Officer Amelia Edwards: 

> *"How can ChemCorp prevent the loss of future market share through the identification of future growth industries and the identification of at least one divestment product?"*

## 🎯 Strategic Focus Areas
Following leadership's directive, the analysis bypasses heavy technical jargon to focus on three core business pillars:

### A) Customer Strategy (Understanding Customers)
*   **Size:** Identifying which of the 6 target industries hold the largest volume of customers.
*   **Regionality:** Mapping out which geographic regions generate the majority of corporate revenue.
*   **Seasonality:** Isolating large revenue dips or surges across the months to identify calendar risks.

### B) Market Opportunities (Future Growth)
*   **Revenue Mapping:** Finding which industry vertical currently feeds the most revenue to ChemCorp.
*   **Profit Margins vs. Scale:** Cross-referencing industry profit margins against the density of available business customers to pinpoint high-value upcoming markets.

### C) Product Divestment (Capital Optimization)
*   Evaluating overall profitability across ChemCorp's **6 core industries** and **11 distinct chemical products** to recommend exactly which underperforming category to eliminate.

## 🏢 Business Scope Analyzed
The dataset spans across ChemCorp's entire operational matrix:
*   **Industries:** Aerospace & Defence, Biotechnology, Machinery and Industrial Goods, Medical Equipment, Paper and Packaging, Pharmaceuticals.
*   **Chemical Portfolio:** Chemicals A through K (including Speciality lines A, G, and J).
